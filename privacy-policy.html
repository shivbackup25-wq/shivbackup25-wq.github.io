2. Data stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game stores its save game locally on your device (using Android app storage through Capacitor Preferences and the app's web storage). It contains your game progress, settings, unlocked items and in-game purchases, technical records of completed purchases (for example order or transaction identifiers, so a purchase is never credited twice) and your consent-related settings.

  • This data is not sent to us. We have no server that receives it and cannot see it.
  • Storing it is necessary to provide the game you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR; § 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act (TKG 2021)).
  • You can delete it at any time by resetting the game in its settings, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the game.
  • If you have enabled Android's backup, Android may include the app's data in your device backup in your Google account. This happens under Google's terms and settings; we have no access to it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

PixelFab is free to play and financed by ads. We use Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (for users in the EEA and Switzerland) and Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA. The game shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch and occasional interstitial ads between game moments. Players who bought "No Ads" see no interstitial ads.

What the AdMob SDK processes

Device identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID and similar identifiers
IP addressUsed to deliver ads and to estimate your approximate location (country/region), not your precise location
Device informationDevice model, operating system version, language, screen size, app name and version
Ad interactionsAd requests, impressions, taps, video views, app launches
DiagnosticsTechnical performance data of the ad SDK (for example load times)

Google uses this data to deliver and measure ads, limit how often you see an ad, detect and prevent fraud and invalid clicks, and, only if you consent, to show ads personalised to your interests. Data is transmitted in encrypted form (TLS).

Your consent

If you are in the EU/EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) before personalised ads are requested. The form lists Google's advertising partners and lets you agree, refuse, or choose individual purposes. Refusing has no effect on the game except the kind of ads you see.

  • Change or withdraw your choice at any time: in the game, open Settings → Privacy options. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before.
  • If you do not consent, Google may show non-personalised or "limited" ads, which Google designs to work with less data. Your IP address is still processed to deliver the ad, to determine your approximate region and to prevent fraud.
  • You can also reset or delete your advertising ID on your phone (Android Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device) and manage ad personalisation in your Google account at My Ad Center.

Legal basis

  • Personalised ads and storing or reading information on your device for advertising: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG).
  • Delivering non-personalised or limited ads, measuring them and preventing fraud: our legitimate interest in financing a free game and in protecting it against abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), to the extent no consent is required.

Google processes this data partly as an independent controller. More information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. In-app purchases (Google Play)

You can buy optional items (crystal packs and one-time unlocks such as "No Ads"). All payments are processed by Google Play's billing system under the Google Play Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

  • We never receive your payment card or bank details.
  • The game receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play (for example product ID, order or purchase token and time) and stores it on your device to unlock the item and to restore one-time unlocks after reinstalling. The game does not send it to us.
  • In Google Play Console we can see order information such as the order number, product, price, date and country, which we use for accounting, refunds and support.

Legal basis: performance of the purchase contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legal obligations to keep accounting records (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR together with Austrian tax and commercial law (e.g. § 132 BAO)).

5. Google Play services

You download PixelFab through Google Play, which processes data under Google's own privacy policy. In this context we receive from Google:

  • Aggregated statistics (for example number of installs, countries, device types, retention), which do not identify you.
  • Crash and performance reports ("Android vitals") if you have allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google. These contain technical information such as device model, Android version and the error trace, but no name or contact details.
  • Reviews you choose to publish on Google Play. The game may occasionally show Google Play's in-app review dialog; your rating and review are handled by Google, and we only see what is published.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running, improving and fixing the game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

9. Children

PixelFab is intended for players aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 16 (or under 13 in the United States). If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal data to us, for example by e-mail, contact us and we will delete it.
